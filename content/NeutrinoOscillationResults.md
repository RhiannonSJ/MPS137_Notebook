## 3. Discussion and Outlook

1. **CP Violation and $\delta_{\text{CP}}$:** Joint analysis between T2K and NOvA shows varying degrees of tension in preferred values of $\delta_{\text{CP}}$ under Normal Ordering. Combined analyses slightly favour Inverted Ordering or values of $\delta_{\text{CP}} \sim 270^\circ$, though CP conservation ($\delta_{\text{CP}} = 0, 180^\circ$) remains allowed within $1\sigma\text{--}2\sigma$ depending on the treatment of atmospheric data.
2. **Mass Ordering:** Normal ordering is preferred by global data at roughly $2.5\sigma\text{--}3\sigma$, with upcoming medium-baseline reactor experiments such as JUNO[^3] poised to decisively resolve the ordering.
3. **$\theta_{23}$ Octant:** The atmospheric angle remains consistent with maximal mixing ($\theta_{23} \approx 45^\circ$), though slight preferences exist for the lower octant ($\theta_{23} < 45^\circ$) in Normal Ordering and the upper octant ($\theta_{23} > 45^\circ$) in Inverted Ordering.

[^3]: JUNO Collaboration, *"First Precision Measurements of Reactor Antineutrino Oscillation Parameters"*, Nature (2026).
